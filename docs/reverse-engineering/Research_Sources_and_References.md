# Research sources and references

All sources used for the eight applications, with links and access dates. Accessed 12 November 2026 unless stated. Ids match the citations in the deck, the workbooks and `Application_Process_Flows.md`.

## How to read this

- **Vendor site / help / AppExchange / release notes**: primary evidence (Verified).
- **Snippet**: the page returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches (G2, Capterra, some vendor sites); the fact comes from a search-engine snippet of that page.
- **Third party**: directories, press and competitor pages; used for context and marked as such.
- Screenshots and demo videos are linked, not copied.

## Research limitations

- No product was installed; internals are known only where vendors publish names.
- Help content for some products is old (Propertybase 2017–2021, Real Properties 2008–2019).
- Picklist and stage values are vendor defaults or examples and may differ in customer orgs.
- Real Properties was identified as the only exact-name AppExchange match (Real Intelligence, Inc.); its functional detail is thin.
