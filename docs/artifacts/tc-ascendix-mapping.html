AscendixRE to TC Commercial Real Estate

What the AscendixRE app contains, what we can rebuild and deploy to the REA demo org under the TC_ naming convention, and what we can't rebuild or access. Everything here comes from AscendixRE's public help centre and AppExchange listing. The package isn't installed in our org, and none of its source code was viewed.

Data model fidelity

How much of the AscendixRE data model can be copied

The share of each layer that AscendixRE documents publicly. That's the part TC can reproduce as it is. Everything else TC fills in with standard commercial real estate design, labelled as such.

Objects (names, purpose)~95%
Relationships~70%
Fields (labels)~30%
Picklist values~25%
Field API names~2%
Overall (weighted)~30%

Fields and picklists make up most of a data model and are the least documented, which pulls the overall figure down. AscendixRE doesn't publish its total field count, so these figures are estimates. API names would differ anyway, because every TC component uses the TC_ prefix rather than AscendixRE's ascendix__ namespace.

At a glance

In the app, deployable as TC, out of reach

What AscendixRE includes

  • Property-centred CRM: Properties, Availabilities, Listings, Leases, Sales, Deals, Inquiries
  • 12 deal business lines with a Close Deal process that creates Leases, Sales or Listings
  • Rent tables (Deal and Lease Periods) with rent calculation and review
  • Commissions: inside broker, analyst, co-broker and expense records, fee methods, split rules, privacy
  • Capital Markets: Deal Sources, Capital Sources, Term Sheets, multi-property deals
  • Stacking plan with PDF and Excel export
  • Ascendix Search: map shapes, saved searches, ad-hoc lists, bulk actions, mass email
  • Composer document generation: PDF, HTML email, microsites
  • Admin Console: field mappings, automation toggles, naming formats, web forms
  • MarketSpace portal and AI Suite add-ons

What TC can deploy

  • 21 TC_ objects with the same names, purpose and documented relationships
  • Every documented field and picklist value; gaps filled with standard CRE design
  • 12 Deal record types, 4 Commission record types, 3 Listing record types
  • Close Deal, client auto-fill, Inquiry conversion, rent-table generation in Apex
  • Commission engine: Percent, Amount, $ per SF; split ≤ 100%; privacy through permissions
  • Lease expiration reminders at 18, 12 and 6 months (Flow)
  • Custom LWCs: stacking plan, image viewer, scorecards, editable grids, map search, Kanban pipeline
  • PDF documents: property flyer, tour book, lease comp report
  • Settings in custom metadata, mirroring the Admin Console options

What TC can't deploy or access

  • AscendixRE source code (Apex, LWC, Flows): protected in the managed package, and not installed
  • The exact field list, API names and undocumented picklist values
  • Branding, product names and UI designs (trademark and copyright)
  • Composer's template designer, microsites and Mailchimp email
  • MarketSpace portal and LoopNet or Crexi feeds
  • AI Suite: AI Agent, Harvest, Composer AI, xRE Connector
  • Polygon drawing on Google Maps or OpenStreetMap
  • AppExchange perk: package objects that don't count against org limits

Installing the package to extract its schema would likely breach AscendixRE's licence terms, so we treat that route as closed.

Bottom line

TC can match AscendixRE's object structure almost one for one, with 21 objects, and fully cover 6 of the 14 modules. About 30% of the data model's detail is documented, so TC copies that much exactly and builds the rest with standard design. Proprietary code, the exact schema, branding and the paid add-ons stay out of reach.
